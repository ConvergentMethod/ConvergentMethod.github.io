Welcome to the Convergence of Excellence.

Convergent Method was founded on a special vision: to unify multidisciplinary practices rarely housed within a single firm. By integrating the specialized expertise of general contractors, professional engineers, and healthcare providers, we deliver a cohesive, end-to-end delivery model tailored to complex facilities.

Our integrated approach guides clients through every phase of the project lifecycle: from initial concept and planning through construction execution and sustainable, long-term operations. This comprehensive oversight optimizes building performance, reduces lifecycle maintenance costs, and ensures safe, functional environments for staff, patients, and visitors alike.

At Convergent Method, our guiding operational standard remains simple: Know where you're going before you get there.
